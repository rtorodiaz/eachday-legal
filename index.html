Settings

Private by default

Your habits are yours.

No account, no analytics, no tracking. Your data lives on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if you turn sync on.

Stored on your device

Habits, entries, notes and streaks are saved locally. There is no Take Form server holding your data.

No tracking

No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. We never ask for your email or location.

Optional iCloud sync

Keep your devices in step through your own private iCloud. Off by default, one toggle to turn on.

Apple Health, read-only

Connect a habit and Take Form reads the metric to fill in your day. It never writes to Health.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need an account?

No. Take Form works the moment you open it. There is nothing to sign up for and no email to give.

Is it free?

Yes. Free includes three habits with widgets, reminders and notes. Pro, inside the app, removes the limit and adds Apple Health sync, advanced stats, Vacation Mode and export.

What happens to my data if I go back to Free?

Nothing is deleted. Your habits and history stay on your phone. You keep tracking up to three habits for free and choose which ones stay active.

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes. Steps, exercise minutes, sleep, water, calories, distance, stand hours and more can fill in a habit automatically. Take Form only reads from Health, it never writes to it.

Can I use it on iPad or Mac?

Take Form is designed for iPhone. It installs on iPad in iPhone-compatibility mode. A dedicated iPad layout is on the roadmap.

Can I export my data?

Pro includes export to JSON or CSV at any time from Settings. Your data is never locked in.
