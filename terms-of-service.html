Free and Pro

The App offers a free tier, which includes up to three active habits, and a paid tier, Take Form Pro, with additional features such as unlimited habits, Apple Health sync, advanced statistics, Vacation Mode and data export. If a Pro subscription ends, nothing is deleted: your habits and history stay on your device, and you choose which three habits remain active on the free tier.

Subscriptions & Purchases

Take Form Pro is available as an auto-renewable subscription (weekly, monthly or yearly) or as a one-time lifetime purchase. All purchases are managed through Apple's App Store:

  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Free trials, when offered, convert to a paid subscription at the end of the trial unless cancelled before it ends. Trial eligibility is determined by Apple and is limited to one per Apple Account.
  • Lifetime purchase is a one-time, non-recurring payment.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your device's Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions, or at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple according to their refund policy.

Your Data

You own your data. The App stores habit data locally on your device and optionally syncs it via your personal iCloud account. You can export (with Pro) or delete all your data at any time from within the App. See our Privacy Policy for details on how data is handled.

Apple Health Integration

If you choose to connect habits to Apple Health, the App reads health data according to the permissions you grant. You can revoke access at any time in your device's Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices. The App never writes data to Apple Health. Apple Health sync is a Pro feature; habits created from Health templates on the free tier are tracked manually.
