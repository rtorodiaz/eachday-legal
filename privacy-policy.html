Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 20, 2026

EachDay ("the App") is a habit tracking app built by Ricardo Toro Díaz. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it's used, and your choices.

Data We Collect

Habit data you create — habit names, goals, frequencies, completion entries, notes, streaks, badges, tags, and wildcards. All of this is stored locally on your device.

App preferences — theme, accent color, sound settings, notification times, and layout options. Stored locally on your device.

Apple Health data (optional) — if you choose to connect a habit to Apple Health, the App reads health metrics (steps, exercise minutes, sleep, calories, distance, water intake) to auto-populate your habit entries. The App only reads data from Apple Health — it never writes to it. This data stays on your device.

Purchase information — if you subscribe to EachDay Pro, purchase and entitlement status is processed through RevenueCat, our subscription management service. RevenueCat receives only purchase-related data (subscription type, status, transaction receipts). It does not receive your habit data or app usage.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require an account, email, or personal information to use the App.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not collect device identifiers for tracking purposes.
  • We do not collect crash reports or telemetry.

iCloud Sync

The App offers optional iCloud sync using Apple's CloudKit. When enabled, your habit data is synced across your devices through your personal iCloud account. This data is stored in Apple's iCloud infrastructure under your Apple Account and is not accessible to us. You can disable iCloud sync at any time in Settings.

Notifications

All notifications are local — they are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. No notification data is sent to external servers.

Data Export & Deletion

You can export all your data (JSON or CSV) at any time from Settings. You can also delete all your data from within the App. When you delete the App, all locally stored data is removed from your device.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party service:

  • RevenueCat — for managing in-app subscriptions and purchases. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

No other third-party services, analytics tools, or advertising networks are used.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App does not require any personal information to function.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
