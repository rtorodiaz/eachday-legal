Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Take Form ("the App") is a habit tracker for iPhone built by the Take Form team. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data the App handles, how it's used, and your choices.

Data We Collect

Habit data you create — habit names, icons and colors, goals, frequencies, start and end dates, completion entries, skips, pauses, notes, timer sessions and streaks. All of this is stored locally on your device.

App preferences — theme, behavior settings and reminder times. Stored locally on your device.

Apple Health data (optional) — if you choose to connect a habit to Apple Health, the App reads the related metric (steps, exercise minutes, sleep, active calories, distance, water, time in daylight, protein, flights climbed, mindful minutes, stand hours or workouts) to fill in your habit entries. The App only reads data from Apple Health — it never writes to it. This data stays on your device and is never shared with us or with third parties.

Purchase information — if you subscribe to Take Form Pro, purchase and entitlement status is processed through RevenueCat, our subscription management service. RevenueCat receives only purchase-related data (subscription type, status, transaction receipts) together with an anonymous app-generated identifier. It does not receive your habit data, your health data or your app usage.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require an account, email, or personal information to use the App.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not collect device identifiers for tracking purposes.
  • We do not collect crash reports or telemetry.

iCloud Sync

The App offers optional iCloud sync using Apple's CloudKit. When enabled, your habit data is synced across your devices through your personal iCloud account. This data is stored in Apple's iCloud infrastructure under your Apple Account and is not accessible to us. You can disable iCloud sync at any time in Settings > Data & Sync.

Notifications

All reminders are local notifications — they are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. No notification data is sent to external servers.

Widgets and Live Activities

Home Screen widgets and the timer's Live Activity read your habit data from the App's local storage on your device. Nothing is transmitted.

Data Export & Deletion

You can export your data (JSON or CSV) from Settings > Data & Sync with Take Form Pro, and you can delete all your data from within the App at any time. When you delete the App, all locally stored data is removed from your device. Data synced to iCloud can be removed by deleting it in the App with iCloud sync enabled, or from your iCloud storage settings.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party service:

  • RevenueCat — for managing in-app subscriptions and purchases. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Purchases themselves are processed by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. No other third-party services, analytics tools, or advertising networks are used.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App does not require any personal information to function.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@takeform.app.
